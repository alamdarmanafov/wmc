# 01 · Product overview

## The problem
Millions of Muslims move to a new city, start university, relocate for work or travel — and face the same feeling:

> "I don't know anyone here."

Google Maps finds a mosque. Instagram shows people. Facebook/WhatsApp groups exist. None of them answers:
**"Find the Muslim community in my city that matches my interests."**

## Value proposition
**New city? Find your people.**
Choose a city → pick interests → say what you're looking for → WMC shows matching **people, communities, events and activities**.

## Positioning (important)
- We are **not** a dating app and we never present ourselves as a "Muslim social network".
- The product is **activity-first**: users join *something to do* (football, coffee, study circle) and meet people through it. Home asks *"What do you want to do?"*, not *"Find Muslims"*.
- Tone: modern, warm, premium. Apple / Airbnb / Notion simplicity. No crescent-and-mosque clichés.

## Target audience
**Primary (18–35):** students, expats, newcomers, young professionals, travellers.
**Secondary:** families, entrepreneurs, converts, Islamic organisations and local communities.

## Core principles
1. **People → Communities → Activities → Events** are the only objects in MVP.
2. **Privacy by default.** Exact location is never shown (only "Near you" / "~2 km away" / city). Users can hide location entirely.
3. **Consent before contact.** Connect request → accept → chat. No cold DMs.
4. **Safety from day one.** Report and block on every profile, event, activity and message. Human moderation in the admin panel.
5. **Simple, explainable matching.** Weighted scoring, no AI.

## Success metrics (first 1,000 users)
```
1,000 sign-ups
  → 400 join a community        (40%)
  → 250 join an event/activity  (25%)
  → 150 make a connection       (15%)
```
The single question the MVP must answer: **Can we get people to meet people?**
Track: onboarding completion, D7/D30 retention, community joins, event joins, accepted connections, messages per accepted connection, reports per 1,000 users.

## User flow
```
Download → Sign up → Choose city → Profile → Interests → What you're looking for
→ "We found 12 people & 8 communities for you." → Join community → See event → Join → Meet people
```
